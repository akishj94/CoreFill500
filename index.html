Thermal resistance
Sealed aminoplast cells trap air — delivering superior R-values across all CMU block densities. Outperforms loose-fill perlite and zonolite, which settle over time. Core-Fill 500 does not settle — ever.
Fire safety + biological resistance
No polystyrenes, polyurethane, or petrochemicals. Class A / ASTM E-84 — flame spread 0, smoke developed 5. A trace of boric acid in the resin prevents mold, mildew, bacteria growth, and pest nesting. Emits zero toxic gases during or after installation.
Acoustic shielding
The foam structure absorbs sound vibrations, significantly reducing sound transmission through masonry walls. Specified in schools, theaters, animal kennels, and recording studios where STC performance matters.
